Advanced Product Specifications & Protective Features

Optimized visual performance with protection against workplace eye hazards

EUROPULL C8 safety spectacles are constructed to optimize visual performance while mitigating workplace eye hazards. Every detail of the eyewear is designed with durability and precise protection in mind.

Maximum Coverage

Engineered with full frame protection to ensure comprehensive coverage of the eye area.

Advanced Lens Technology

Features polarized lenses that help reduce reflected glare and significantly improve visual comfort in bright working environments.

Ultimate UV Defense

The lenses act as filters for 99.9% of harmful UV rays, offering robust protection against UVA and UVB radiation.

Anti-UV Treatment

Specialized anti-UV treatment helps maintain visibility and protects the wearer against harmful rays and rapidly changing environmental conditions.

Enhanced Durability

Delivers high optical quality for clear, comfortable vision during extended periods of use.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What level of UV protection do the EUROPULL C8 spectacles offer?

The EUROPULL C8 safety spectacles feature lenses that filter 99.9% of harmful UV rays, providing comprehensive protection against UVA and UVB radiation.

Are the C8 safety glasses comfortable for all-day use?

Yes, the C8 model features a lightweight ergonomic frame specifically designed for comfortable occupational wear and high optical quality for clear vision during extended use.
