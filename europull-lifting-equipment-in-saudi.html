What is the difference between a Europull manual chain block and a lever hoist?

A manual chain block is operated by pulling a hand chain, making it ideal for vertical lifting operations. A lever hoist uses a ratcheting lever handle, allowing operators to lift, tension, or pull loads horizontally or at specific angles, which is highly useful in pipe alignment and structural fabrication.
