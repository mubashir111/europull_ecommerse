Frequently Asked Questions

Key technical and commercial answers regarding Europull winch capabilities, local certifications, and selection guidance

Who distributes Europull winches in Saudi Arabia?

Onshore Technical Supplies CO is the authorized distribution partner for Europull Hebentechnik UK Ltd in Saudi Arabia. With our central warehouse in Dammam and logistics corridors serving Riyadh and Jeddah, we maintain local stocks of HWV hydraulic, HWE industrial, and EWB electric winch models for swift dispatch across the Kingdom.

What is the maximum pulling capacity of Europull industrial winches?

The Europull HWE Hydraulic Winch Series offers exceptional power, with the flagship HWE 300 model providing a maximum rated line pull of 66,000 lbs (30,000 kgs). For heavy commercial vehicle applications, our HWV hydraulic series pulls up to 15,000 lbs (6,804 kgs), and our EWB 24V electric series delivers up to 25,000 lbs (11,340 kgs).

Are Europull winches suitable for wet or marine environments?

Yes. All Europull winch models across the HWV, HWE, and EWB series feature full IP67 ingress waterproofing. This seals internal planetary gears, electrical contactors, and motors against saltwater spray, dust storms, high humidity, and pressure washing.

What type of braking systems do these winches use?

The HWV and HWE hydraulic series utilize highly reliable wet-type hydraulic multi-disc brakes that deliver 100% load holding without overheating. The EWB electric series features Europull’s patented mechanical ratchet braking system, preventing load slippage under full capacity in accordance with European EN 14492-1 safety mandates.

What are the specifications of the EWB electric winch series?

The EWB series features heavy-duty 24V DC series-wound electric motors, 3-stage hardened planetary steel gears, ergonomic manual clutches, waterproof control packs, and line pulls ranging from 12,500 lbs (5,670 kgs) to 25,000 lbs (11,340 kgs).

Do Europull winches meet international safety standards?

Yes. All Europull HWV, HWE, and EWB winch models are manufactured in strict compliance with European EN standards (including EN 14492-1 for power-driven winches) and CE directives. Each unit is individually inspected and proof-tested prior to release.

How can I order Europull winches in Riyadh, Dammam, or Jeddah?

You can request a direct quote through this website by clicking the "Request Quote" button on any winch model, which triggers our online RFQ system. Alternatively, contact our sales desk directly via email at INFO@EUROPULL.COM or call our regional office for delivery arrangements across KSA.

Which Europull winch do I need for my recovery truck?

Winch selection depends on your vehicle’s Gross Vehicle Weight Rating (GVWR) and intended service. For light 3-ton to 5-ton tow trucks, the HWV 10000 or HWV 12000 models are ideal. For 7-ton to 10-ton recovery beds, the HWV 15000 hydraulic or EWB 25000 electric series deliver optimal capacity. For heavy 15-ton to 30-ton recovery rotators, our industrial HWE series (HWE 150 to HWE 300) delivers massive pulling force. If you are replacing an older winch model (such as a Runva or competitor unit), our engineering specialists can cross-reference your mounting bolt patterns and hydraulic flow rates.
