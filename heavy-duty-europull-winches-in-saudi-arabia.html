The Europull Engineering Advantage

Global engineering excellence in towing, maritime, mining, and oilfield environments since 1940

Since 1940, Europull Hebentechnik UK Ltd has been synonymous with uncompromising strength in the field of heavy-duty pulling, lifting, and recovery. As a premier manufacturer headquartered in the United Kingdom, we engineer industrial winches designed to execute complex operations in the world's most extreme towing, marine, mining, and construction environments.

From the towering offshore platforms of the Gulf of Mexico to high-tech shipyards and rigorous marine hubs globally, Europull Hebentechnik UK Ltd delivers engineering excellence. Our team of highly skilled specialists understands that in the oilfield, maritime, and heavy fabrication sectors, there is no room for error.

Our manufacturing capabilities feature precision machining, advanced welding, and custom fabrication, ensuring every winch we produce exceeds the most stringent international safety standards, including CE, EN, and ASME. Europull Hebentechnik UK Ltd equipment is systematically specified for use in highly regulated project environments where safety, durability, and sustained performance are the ultimate priorities.

All Europull equipment is engineered to exceed stringent international safety standards, fully complying with CE, EN, and ASME certifications.

What is the maximum pulling capacity of your industrial winches?

The Europull HWE Hydraulic Winch Series offers exceptional power, with the flagship HWE 300 model providing a maximum rated line pull of 66,000 lbs (30,000 kgs).

Are these winches suitable for wet or marine environments?

Yes. All winch models across the HWV, HWE, and EWB series feature IP67 waterproofing, making them highly resistant to dust, moisture, and water ingress in challenging offshore, shipyard, or mining environments.

What type of braking systems do these winches use?

The HWV and HWE hydraulic series utilize highly reliable wet-type hydraulic brakes, while the EWB electric series features a patented ratchet braking system for secure load holding.

Which winch do I need for my recovery truck or industrial setup?

Your winch selection depends on your vehicle’s payload. For light 3-ton to 5-ton trucks, the HWV 10000 or 12000 models are ideal. For medium 7-ton to 10-ton heavy-duty applications, the HWV 15000 or EWB electric series provide perfect capacity. For heavy-duty 15-ton to 30-ton recovery rotators, the industrial HWE series delivers massive pulling power.
